A galaxy is a large group of stars, gas, and dust held together by gravity. We live in the Milky Way.

Galaxies can be spiral, elliptical, or irregular in shape. They formed after the Big Bang. Studying galaxies helps us understand the universe and how it began.

  • PROJECT A - Types of Galaxies Spiral, elliptical, irregular, and lenticular galaxies each have unique shapes. They differ in star populations, size, and structure across the universe.
  • PROJECT B - Famous Galaxies Discover Andromeda, Sombrero, Messier 87, and the Magellanic Clouds. Each has fascinating traits and stories that captivate astronomers.
  • PROJECT C - Galaxies and Black Holes Most galaxies host supermassive black holes at their centers. These powerful forces shape stars, gas, and galactic evolution.
